## Narrative Arc

The chapter traces how psychology evolved during the Patristic period (roughly 2nd–5th centuries CE) as Christianity absorbed, transformed, and sometimes distorted classical philosophy. It moves from debunking the "Dark Ages" myth, through the convergence of pagan and Christian thought, to Augustine's revolutionary contributions to epistemology and ethics, and closes with Galen's parallel scientific tradition.
