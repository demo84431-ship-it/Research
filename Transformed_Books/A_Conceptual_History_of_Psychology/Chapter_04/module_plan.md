### Module 5: Materialism, Empiricism, and the Reflex
**Source lines:** 1441–2120 | **Source words:** ~9,200 | **Concepts:** 5

| # | Concept | Source |
|---|---------|--------|
| 1 | Spinoza's monism — one substance, mind and body as two faces of the same coin | Spinoza section |
| 2 | The mind-body solutions — occasionalism, parallelism, compared | Malebranche + Leibniz |
| 3 | La Mettrie — "Man a Machine," materialism and human virtue | La Mettrie section |

**Key terms to define:** monism, occasionalism, neutral parallelism, materialism, empiricism, hedonism, individualism, reflex, stimulus, conditioned response
