5 Conclusion
This paper has offered a critical reflection on the concept of saturation and its use in
qualitative research, contributing to the small body of literature that has examined the
complexities of the concept and its underlying assumptions. Drawing on recent examples
of its use, saturation has been discussed in relation to three key sets of questions: What?
Where and why? When and how?
