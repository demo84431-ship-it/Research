# Chapter 42: Theoretical Frameworks

## Why This Matters

A theoretical framework is the lens through which you view your research problem. It connects your study to existing knowledge and provides a structure for interpreting your findings.

---

## What Is a Theoretical Framework?

A theoretical framework is an existing theory (or set of theories) that:
- Explains the phenomenon you are studying
- Guides your research questions
- Shapes your methodology
- Provides a lens for interpreting results

---

## How to Choose a Framework

### Step 1: Identify Relevant Theories
From your literature review, note which theories other researchers have used.

### Step 2: Evaluate Fit
Ask:
- Does this theory explain the relationship between my variables?
- Has it been used in similar studies?
- Does it align with my research questions?

### Step 3: Justify Your Choice
Explain:
- What the theory says
- Why it is appropriate for your study
- How it connects to your research questions

---

## Common Frameworks by Discipline

| Discipline | Common Frameworks |
|---|---|
| Psychology | Social Cognitive Theory, Theory of Planned Behavior, Self-Determination Theory |
| Education | Constructivism, Connectivism, Bloom's Taxonomy |
| Business | Resource-Based View, Institutional Theory, Stakeholder Theory |
| Health | Health Belief Model, Theory of Reasoned Action, Transtheoretical Model |
| Sociology | Structural Functionalism, Conflict Theory, Symbolic Interactionism |

---

## Common Mistakes

1. **No framework** — You describe your methods but never explain the theoretical lens
2. **Wrong framework** — The theory doesn't match your research questions
3. **Framework is decoration** — You mention a theory but never use it to guide your study
4. **Too many frameworks** — Using 3–4 theories without integrating them

---

## Checklist

- [ ] I have identified a relevant theory
- [ ] The theory connects to my research questions
- [ ] I have explained what the theory says
- [ ] I have justified why this theory is appropriate
- [ ] The theory guides my methodology and interpretation
