# Mechanistic Interpretability

> **Understanding what models are "thinking"**

---

## What Is It?

Mechanistic interpretability aims to understand the internal workings of neural networks — not just what they output, but *how* and *why* they produce specific outputs.

---

## Key Concepts

- **Circuits:** Algorithms learned by neural networks
- **Features:** Concepts represented by individual neurons or directions in activation space
- **Attribution graphs:** Visual representations of how models process information
- **Monosemanticity:** When a neuron represents one concept
- **Polysemanticity:** When a neuron represents multiple concepts
