# Scalable Oversight

> **Keeping AI honest as it surpasses human intelligence**

---

## What Is It?

Scalable oversight develops techniques to ensure AI systems remain helpful and honest even as they become more capable than humans in many domains.

---

## Key Approaches

1. **Debate:** Two AI systems argue; humans judge who's right
2. **IDA (Iterated Distillation and Amplification):** Break complex tasks into simpler subtasks
3. **Recursive Reward Modeling:** Weak supervisors train stronger supervisors
4. **Weak-to-strong generalization:** Can a weak model supervise a stronger one?
