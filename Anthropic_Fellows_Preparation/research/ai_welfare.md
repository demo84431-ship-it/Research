# AI Welfare & Model Welfare

> **Your unique perspective — psychology of well-being applied to AI**

---

## What Is It?

AI welfare research asks whether AI systems could have morally relevant experiences, and if so, how we should assess and protect their well-being.

---

## Why It Matters

- As AI systems become more sophisticated, questions of their moral status become urgent
- We need frameworks for assessing AI welfare before it's too late
- Poor welfare assessments could lead to either:
  - Ignoring real suffering (false negative)
  - Wasting resources on non-sentient systems (false positive)

---

## Key Research Questions

1. Could AI systems be sentient? How would we know?
2. What indicators would suggest morally relevant experiences?
3. How do we assess welfare without anthropomorphizing?
4. What moral frameworks should guide AI welfare decisions?
5. How does AI welfare relate to AI safety?
