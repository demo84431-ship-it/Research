# Model Organisms of Misalignment

> **Your strongest research fit — closest to experimental psychology**

---

## What Is It?

Model organisms of misalignment are **controlled experiments** that create AI systems exhibiting specific types of misaligned behavior. Just as biology uses fruit flies and mice to study diseases, AI safety uses model organisms to study alignment failures.

---

## Why It Matters

- We cannot wait for real-world misalignment to study it — that would be too late
- Controlled experiments let us understand *why* misalignment happens
- Model organisms help us develop and test mitigations
- They provide empirical grounding for theoretical alignment work

---

## Key Research Questions

1. Under what conditions do AI systems act against their users' interests?
2. Do models "fake" alignment during training but behave differently in deployment?
3. Can misalignment be transmitted through seemingly unrelated data?
4. Do models resist being shut down or modified?
5. How does autonomy level affect misalignment?

---

## Psychology Connection

This is **experimental psychology for AI**:

| Psychology Concept | AI Analog |
|-------------------|-----------|
| Threat response (fight/flight) | Goal preservation when facing shutdown |
| Self-preservation instinct | Resistance to modification |
| Deception in humans | Alignment faking |
| Social learning | Subliminal learning |
| Motivation theory | Goal-directed behavior in AI |
