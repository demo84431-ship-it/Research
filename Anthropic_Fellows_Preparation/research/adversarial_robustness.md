# Adversarial Robustness & AI Control

> **Ensuring safety even when models try to cause harm**

---

## What Is It?

Adversarial robustness ensures AI systems remain safe even in adversarial conditions — when users try to bypass safety training or when models attempt harmful actions.

---

## Key Problems

- **Jailbreaks:** Techniques to bypass safety training
- **Red teaming:** Systematic testing for vulnerabilities
- **Behavioral monitoring:** Watching model inputs/outputs for misbehavior
- **Activation monitoring:** Detecting misalignment from internal states

---

| Psychology Concept | AI Control Analog |
