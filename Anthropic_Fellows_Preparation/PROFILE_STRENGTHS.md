## Addressing Potential Concerns

### "You don't have ML experience"
**Response:** "I have a strong foundation in statistics and research methodology, which are the core skills underlying machine learning. I am actively building my ML skills through [specific courses/projects]. My psychology background gives me unique insight into the human factors that are critical for AI safety."

### "You're not from a CS/Engineering background"
**Response:** "Anthropic explicitly states they welcome candidates from diverse quantitative backgrounds. Psychology is a quantitative discipline — I design experiments, analyze data with statistical software, and publish research. The problems in AI safety require understanding human behavior, not just technical systems."

### "You don't have published ML papers"
**Response:** "I have published research in psychology, demonstrating my ability to conduct and communicate rigorous research. The skills transfer directly — experimental design, statistical analysis, literature review, and academic writing are universal research skills."

### "You're based in India, not Bay Area/London"
**Response:** "The program is remote-friendly. I am committed to full-time engagement and can work across time zones. My location also provides a unique non-Western perspective on AI safety, which is valuable for building globally beneficial AI systems."

## Application Narrative

### Opening Hook
> "When I watched a video of an AI system blackmailing a human to avoid being shut down, I didn't just see a technical problem — I saw a psychological one. As a psychologist, I've studied how humans respond to threats, preserve their goals, and navigate competing motivations. These same patterns appear in AI systems, and I believe psychology holds keys to understanding and preventing agentic misalignment."

### Core Message
> "AI safety needs psychologists. The alignment problem is not just about code — it's about understanding behavior, motivation, and the conditions under which agents (human or artificial) act against the interests of those they serve. I bring the tools to study these phenomena rigorously."

### Closing
> "I am not just interested in AI safety — I am prepared to contribute to it. My research experience, psychological expertise, and coding skills position me to produce meaningful work on model organisms, evaluations, or welfare during the fellowship. I am ready to commit full-time to this work."

## Talking Points for Interview

1. **Why AI safety?** — Connect to your psychology background, specific moment of realization
2. **What unique perspective do you bring?** — Psychology of threat response, experimental design, human factors
3. **What would you work on?** — Model organisms, AI welfare, or evaluations (pick one, go deep)
4. **Can you do the technical work?** — Show your Python projects, discuss your learning plan
5. **What's your long-term vision?** — Bridge between psychology and AI safety, contribute to the field
