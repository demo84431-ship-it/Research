## Reading Strategy

1. **Read the abstract and conclusion first** — get the big picture
2. **Then read the methods** — understand what they actually did
3. **Take notes in your own words** — forces understanding
4. **Connect to your perspective** — how does this relate to psychology?
5. **Write a 3-sentence summary** — practice concise communication
