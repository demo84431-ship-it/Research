There is a largely undefended convention adopted by historians of psychology of presenting rationalism as the nearly mechanical "cause" of empiricism and materialism. On this construal, empiricism is to be understood as a long reply to the claims of the rationalists, and materialism as a refinement of or inference from empiricism. Thus we learn (over and over again) that the "father" of modern empiricism (allegedly John Locke) set out to overturn the theory of "innate ideas" advanced by that patron of modern rationalism, Rene Descartes.
